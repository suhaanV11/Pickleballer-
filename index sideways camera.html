    What this prototype does and does not do

    • It finds the ball by motion, spots the frame where it stops falling and starts rising, and maps that spot onto the court.
    • A ball touching the line is in. Anything inside the too-close zone is reported as too close to call, never as out.
    • You are the check. Watch the replay and mark each call right or wrong. That tally is your accuracy count.
    • A paddle hit low to the ground can look like a bounce. Use Not a bounce to remove it.
    • At 60 frames a second a fast ball moves about 45 cm between frames, so slo-mo recording matters.
